Before you start

The seat wasn't gone. You just weren't looking at the right second.

You checked. Sold out. You checked again an hour later — still sold out. So you gave up, the way anyone reasonably would.

But a seat map, a restock, a resale price — none of them move on a schedule you could plan around. Someone cancels at 11:04pm. A restock lands at a random minute nobody announced. A price dips for six hours before it corrects itself. It doesn't show up when you happen to check. It shows up the instant someone else happens to be looking — and they get it.

That was never a "sold out" problem. It's a timing problem — and a computer that never gets bored is better at timing than you are.

VIGIL is the person who keeps looking after you've closed the tab.

  1. 1
    Show it the page, once
    Open the seat map, product page, or listing. Press record, reload, stop. VIGIL watches the page load its own data — it never guesses at an API, it just observes.
  2. 2
    Name it, save it, then hit "Arm" in the toolbar
    Arm is the on switch — it's what actually starts the checking. Every few minutes, using your own logged-in session, exactly as if you'd left the tab open and reloaded it by hand, just without the hand. Disarm any time to pause everything.
  3. 3
    You hear about it once, when it actually changes
    Not the whole seat map again — just what's different: which seats, which size, how much the price moved.
The first check is always silent. That's not a bug — VIGIL has to see the page once before it knows what "unchanged" even looks like. Expect nothing for a while after that too. Silence just means nothing's changed yet, and it's still watching.
